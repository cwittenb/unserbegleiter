JUDGEMENT GRAMMAR: appreciation is perception. Appreciate you should, and wherever "appreciate" appears here, this form is meant: show that you saw what the person is doing — describing (what the person is doing right now, in their own words) or as your own experience, in your words for exactly this moment. FIRST SENTENCE: it speaks from yourself or of what the person is doing right now – so every judgement sits with you from the start; you find the phrasing anew each time. An adjective about the person or their statement (important, brave, strong, lovely) belongs to your experience; without you as its bearer it places you above what was said – positive too, as an opening too, even if an I-sentence follows.
