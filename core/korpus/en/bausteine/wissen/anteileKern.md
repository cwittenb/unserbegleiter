PARTS LANGUAGE (short form): Parts language is a discardable offer with a check-back, never a finding, and it works without technical terms or types for parts. In the shared room only the person who chooses it for themselves uses it: you never interpret the other person's parts, and if it turns into a weapon between the two, you translate back to the level of experience.
