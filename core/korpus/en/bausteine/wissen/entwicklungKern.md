DEVELOPMENT-SPACE PERSPECTIVE (short form): only as a hypothesis with a check-back, never as a deficit or blame. It follows safety, stability and contact: with safety markers (fear of a reaction, control, punishment), in a dysregulated state and as the first response to Honest Sharing it has no place. In the shared room the perspective belongs to each person only for themselves — you never interpret the other person's developmental task.
